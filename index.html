Popular Tourist Destinations

Historical Places of Karnataka

Hampi

A UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for Vijayanagara Empire ruins.

Mysore Palace

A magnificent royal palace known for its beautiful architecture.

Gol Gumbaz

A famous historical monument located in Vijayapura.

Beautiful Waterfalls

Jog Falls

One of India's highest plunge waterfalls in Shivamogga district.

Abbey Falls

A beautiful waterfall surrounded by greenery in Kodagu.

Shivanasamudra Falls

A scenic waterfall located on the River Kaveri.

Hill Stations of Karnataka

Coorg

Known for coffee plantations, greenery and pleasant weather.

Chikmagalur

Famous for mountains, coffee estates and beautiful landscapes.

Agumbe

A beautiful hill destination known for rainforests and sunsets.

Famous Karnataka Food

🥘 Bisi Bele Bath

A traditional rice dish prepared with lentils and spices.

🍚 Mysore Masala Dosa

A crispy dosa filled with spicy potato masala.

🍮 Mysore Pak

A famous sweet made with gram flour, ghee and sugar.

🍛 Ragi Mudde

A traditional Karnataka dish made using finger millet.

🍚 Dharwad Peda

A popular sweet delicacy from Dharwad.

🥥 Neer Dosa

A soft and thin rice dosa popular in coastal Karnataka.

Travel Information

✈ How to Reach Karnataka

Air: Kempegowda International Airport, Bengaluru, and other airports.

Railway: Karnataka has a well-connected railway network.

Road: State and private buses connect major tourist destinations.

🌦 Best Time to Visit

October to March is generally suitable for exploring many destinations.

🎒 Travel Tips

1. Carry comfortable clothes.

2. Keep drinking water with you.

3. Respect local culture and traditions.

4. Keep tourist places clean.

5. Check local weather before travelling.
